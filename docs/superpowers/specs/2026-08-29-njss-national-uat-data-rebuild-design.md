# NJSS National UAT Data Rebuild Specification

**Document status:** Approved design specification for user review  
**Date:** 29 August 2026  
**Dataset identifier:** `NJSS-NATIONAL-UAT-2026-V1`  
**Planned run identifier:** `UAT-2026-V1-20260829`  
**System:** National Judiciary Staff Services (NJSS)  
**Database:** NJSS System, Supabase project `qzsmmalfeinoagvronpb`

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines a controlled rebuild of NJSS organisational, financial-master, budget and representative transaction data for national user acceptance testing (UAT).

The rebuild is intended to replace the current mixed test data with a realistic, deterministic and auditable national test dataset covering Waigani headquarters, all 22 province-level jurisdictions, 25 National Court registries and 3 National Court sub-registries represented in the Judiciary's 2026 Court Calendar.

The dataset is not an assertion of the official NJSS establishment, the official Judiciary Chart of Accounts, or actual Government appropriations. Officially evidenced records, logically derived records and synthetic UAT records must remain distinguishable throughout the rebuild and in all generated documentation.

## 2. Scope and reset boundary

### 2.1 Preserve

The reset must preserve the complete security and application-control framework:

- `users`, including authentication links and archived status;
- `roles`;
- `user_roles`;
- `permissions`;
- `role_permissions`;
- user and role data-scope definitions required by RBAC;
- menu items and modules;
- workflow-status definitions;
- report definitions and report categories;
- system settings and system alert settings;
- database schema, functions, triggers, policies and migration history;
- a verified recovery snapshot of the pre-reset database.

The existing 10 user identities are retained. Seven active users remain active. Three currently archived users remain archived and must not be reactivated merely to support UAT.

### 2.2 Rebuild

The following business data classes are rebuilt from zero:

- provinces/jurisdictions;
- court locations and registry types;
- departments/divisions;
- sections;
- cost centres;
- budget divisions;
- projects and relevant business reference masters;
- funding sources and funding transactions;
- expense categories/items;
- Chart of Accounts test hierarchy;
- expense ledger / Finance Codes;
- Posting Codes / expense-code registry;
- canonical finance-posting mappings;
- budget cycles, ceilings, submissions, lines and monthly allocations;
- budget activation batches, lines and snapshots;
- operational budget allocations;
- suppliers and supplier-related UAT data;
- FF3, commitment, FF4 and payment/reconciliation transactions;
- budget revision/supplementary transactions;
- business notifications and business audit history generated by the new UAT dataset.

### 2.3 Destructive-operation control

No destructive reset may execute until all of the following are true:

1. the replacement seed package is complete and internally validated;
2. a fresh pre-reset recovery snapshot is captured and verified;
3. pre-reset table counts are recorded;
4. the implementation plan defines the exact dependency-ordered purge;
5. the preserved user/security datasets are separately reconciled;
6. the reset target is confirmed as Supabase project `qzsmmalfeinoagvronpb`;
7. the implementation plan has been reviewed and approved.

## 3. Source methodology and provenance

Every installed master record must be assigned one provenance classification:

- **OFFICIAL** — directly evidenced by a current Judiciary or PNG Government public source;
- **DERIVED** — a logical organisational subdivision inferred from an officially evidenced function;
- **UAT** — a realistic synthetic record created only for system testing.

The principal source classes are:

1. Judiciary of Papua New Guinea website and the 2026 Court Calendar for court locations, registry/sub-registry status and public descriptions of Judiciary/NJSS functions;
2. Judiciary publications and annual reports for administrative/support functions such as Finance, Human Resources, IT, Registry, Sheriff, Court Reporting, Judicial Support, Infrastructure, Library, Legal, Audit, Procurement, Security and Administration;
3. PNG Department of Finance and Treasury public material for the broad economic-classification structure used as the parent basis of UAT financial coding;
4. current NJSS application/database schema for data-model and validation constraints.

No synthetic NJSS code is to be represented as an official IFMS or Judiciary code.

## 4. Organisational hierarchy

The approved hierarchy is:

`NJSS -> Province/Jurisdiction -> Court Location/Registry -> Department/Division -> Section -> Cost Centre`

This hierarchy is required because the current `provinces` lookup is not sufficient to distinguish repeated functions such as Registry, Sheriff, Administration or Court Reporting across multiple physical court locations.

### 4.1 Court Location master

Introduce a new `court_locations` master. At minimum it shall contain:

- `id` UUID primary key;
- `province_id` UUID foreign key;
- `code` unique business code;
- `name`;
- `location_type`;
- `town`;
- `is_headquarters`;
- `is_active`;
- standard audit timestamps.

Approved `location_type` values are:

- `HEADQUARTERS`;
- `NATIONAL_COURT_REGISTRY`;
- `NATIONAL_COURT_SUB_REGISTRY`.

Operational organisational units must be bound to a Court Location so that repeated functions remain geographically unambiguous.

## 8. Coding standard

### 8.1 Organisational codes

The general convention is:

`[Province]-[Location]-[Function]-[Section]`

Examples:

- `NCD-WGN-FIN-BUD` — Waigani Finance / Budget;
- `NCD-WGN-HR-REC` — Waigani HR / Recruitment;
- `MOR-LAE-REG-CIV` — Lae Registry / Civil;
- `WHP-MHG-SHF-ENF` — Mt Hagen Sheriff / Enforcement;
- `ENB-KOK-CRS-REP` — Kokopo Court Reporting;
- `HEL-TAR-ADM-FIN` — Tari Administration / Finance;
- `ARB-BUK-REG-CRT` — Buka Registry / Courtroom Services;
- `ARB-ARA-REG-CRT` — Arawa Sub-Registry / Courtroom Services.

### 8.2 Cost Centre codes

Cost Centre codes use:

`CC-[Province]-[Location]-[Function]-[Section]`

Examples:

- `CC-NCD-WGN-FIN-BUD`;
- `CC-MOR-LAE-REG-CIV`;
- `CC-WHP-MHG-SHF-ENF`;
- `CC-HEL-TAR-ADM-FIN`.

### 8.3 Stable technical identifiers

Human-readable codes are the business identifiers. Database relationships continue to use UUIDs.

The seed package must use predetermined/deterministic UUIDs for seeded master records so rerunning the same seed does not create random duplicate identities. The rebuild must therefore be repeatable and idempotent for the same dataset version.

## 10. Posting Code and canonical mapping architecture

Posting Codes combine organisational context with the applicable financial classification.

Examples:

- `NCD-WGN-ICT-SYS-27102` — Waigani ICT / Systems / Computer & ICT Equipment;
- `MOR-LAE-REG-CIV-22301` — Lae Registry / Civil / Office Materials.

The required financial chain is:

`Economic CoA -> Finance Ledger -> Posting Code -> Cost Centre -> finance_posting_mappings -> Budget Line -> Operational Allocation -> FF3 -> Commitment -> FF4/Payment`

No default/fallback Chart of Accounts mapping is allowed.

A Posting Code and canonical mapping are generated only where the context is actually required by a budget line or approved UAT test scenario. The rebuild must not generate the Cartesian product of every Finance Code and every Cost Centre.

## 12. Budget model

### 12.1 Location tiers

Budget values are synthetic and deterministic rather than random.

- **Tier H:** Waigani Headquarters;
- **Tier 1:** major regional UAT registries — Lae, Mt Hagen, Kokopo, Wewak, Madang and Goroka;
- **Tier 2:** standard National Court registries;
- **Tier 3:** National Court sub-registries — Bialla, Arawa and Tabubil/Kiunga.

The tier model drives test-data scale only. It does not claim an official Judiciary funding classification.

### 12.2 Monetary-value disclaimer

All monetary values installed by `NJSS-NATIONAL-UAT-2026-V1` are simulated values designed to test system behaviour. They do not represent approved NJSS or Government of Papua New Guinea appropriations.

A standard provincial Registry may contain Finance Codes for travel, accommodation/subsistence, stationery, courtroom materials, vehicle fuel, court operations, judicial circuit support, training, utilities, maintenance and ICT equipment. Values shall be generated consistently from the approved tier and functional profile.

### 12.3 Monthly cash flow

Annual budget lines are allocated monthly using expenditure-specific profiles rather than equal twelfths where a more realistic pattern is useful:

- utilities — relatively even;
- travel/circuits — weighted to planned circuit periods;
- training — concentrated in selected quarters;
- ICT/equipment — concentrated around planned procurement months;
- maintenance — weighted to programmed works.

For every budget line:

`sum(Jan...Dec) = annual approved amount`

with required variance of exactly K0.00.

## 13. Budget workflow and activation

Budgets shall be created through the supported workflow wherever practical:

`Prepared -> Submitted -> Reviewed -> Registrar Approved -> Finance Mapped -> System Administrator Prepared -> Registrar Activated`

Approval is not activation.

Operational `budget_allocations` must be created by the controlled activation mechanism rather than by an arbitrary orphan insert. The dual-control requirement remains:

- System Administrator prepares/validates activation readiness;
- Registrar performs final activation;
- preparer and final authoriser cannot be the same actor.

## 14. Funding model

Representative UAT funding sources may include:

- Government Recurrent;
- Government Development / PIP;
- Development Partner / Donor;
- Special Purpose Funding;
- Other Approved Funding.

Synthetic funding records must be marked UAT unless directly sourced from an actual approved source.

The UAT chain shall exercise:

`Funding Source -> Funding Authority -> Funding Receipt -> Funding Allocation -> Quarterly Release -> Budget Availability`

Funding controls are tested independently from budget/appropriation controls.

## 15. Supplier model

Most supplier records shall be clearly fictitious UAT entities to avoid representing real companies inaccurately. Example naming patterns include Pacific Office Supplies PNG Ltd — UAT, Highlands Fleet Services Ltd — UAT, Coastal Building Maintenance Ltd — UAT and Island ICT Solutions Ltd — UAT.

Supplier scenarios shall cover at least:

- pending;
- verified;
- approved;
- suspended;
- rejected;
- missing-document/incomplete.

No fictitious supplier may be presented as a confirmed registered PNG company.

## 16. Representative transaction centres

Full national master and budget coverage is combined with detailed transactions at a representative set of locations.

### 16.1 Waigani

Exercise the full chain:

`Budget -> Activation -> FF3 -> Procurement -> Commitment -> FF4 -> Payment -> Reconciliation`

Scenarios include ICT equipment, Registry stationery, judicial circuit travel, building maintenance, staff training and Sheriff operational requirements.

Waigani also exercises Supplementary Budget, Virement, Reforecast, Budget Reduction, returned revision and rejected revision cases.

### 16.2 Provincial representative centres

- **Lae:** Registry supplies, judicial circuit travel, Sheriff vehicle/fuel, complete FF3-to-FF4 chain;
- **Mt Hagen:** courthouse maintenance, fuel, operational materials and attempted budget overrun;
- **Wewak:** Court Reporting equipment, ICT support and quotation comparison;
- **Kokopo:** training, travel and supplier workflow;
- **Alotau:** utilities, facilities maintenance and routine FF3/FF4;
- **Tari:** urgent operations, judicial travel, vehicle/fuel and insufficient-funding validation;
- **Buka:** Registry operations, circuit support, funding allocation and regional reporting.

Other locations retain complete organisational and budget master coverage even if they contain fewer transactions.

## 18. Supplementary Budget scenario

A complete UAT scenario shall prove that a supplementary budget never overwrites the original approved baseline.

Illustrative Waigani ICT case:

- Original approved budget: K500,000;
- Approved UAT supplementary adjustment: +K150,000;
- Current revised budget: K650,000.

Required lineage:

`Original -> Supplementary Request -> Line Supervisor Preparation -> Registrar Approval -> Finance Validation -> Revised Operational Allocation`

The original K500,000 baseline remains auditable.

## 19. Virement scenario

A complete virement scenario shall preserve total appropriation.

Illustrative case:

- Building Maintenance before: K180,000;
- Office Equipment before: K100,000;
- transfer: K30,000;
- Building Maintenance after: K150,000;
- Office Equipment after: K130,000.

The test must prove:

`total before = total after`

while preserving both original and revised line history.

## 20. Reset and reload sequence

The implementation plan shall refine the exact table-level order from the live foreign-key graph, but the approved logical sequence is:

1. capture and verify recovery snapshot;
2. record pre-reset table counts and protected-user/security checksums/counts;
3. temporarily detach retained users from old Department/Section references;
4. purge dependent business transactions from the lowest-level children upward;
5. purge old business audit/history only after snapshot verification;
6. remove rebuildable financial and organisational masters after all dependants are cleared;
7. introduce/validate `court_locations` and required binding columns/constraints;
8. load Province/Jurisdiction masters;
9. load Court Locations;
10. load Departments/Divisions;
11. load Sections;
12. load Cost Centres and Budget Divisions;
13. remap retained users to the new hierarchy;
14. load financial/reference masters;
15. load CoA, expense ledger / Finance Codes, Posting Codes and canonical mappings;
16. load budget cycles, ceilings and budgets;
17. take selected budgets through approval and activation;
18. load funding/supplier/reference UAT data;
19. create representative FF3/commitment/FF4/payment/revision scenarios through supported workflows where practical;
20. execute positive and negative validation suites;
21. execute full reconciliation;
22. record final counts and results for the Word implementation/report document.

The implementation must not disable foreign-key integrity globally as a shortcut. If a temporary constraint-management technique is ever required, it must be explicitly justified, transaction-scoped and restored/validated before commit.

## 22. Positive validation and reconciliation

### 22.1 Organisational integrity

Every active operational record must resolve through:

`Province -> Court Location -> Department -> Section -> Cost Centre`

with no orphan or cross-location references.

### 22.2 Financial integrity

Every activated budget context must resolve uniquely through:

`Finance Code -> Expense Ledger -> Posting Code -> CoA -> Cost Centre -> canonical finance_posting_mapping`

There shall be no fallback account selection.

### 22.3 Budget integrity

For each approved budget submission:

- sum of monthly allocations equals the annual line amount;
- sum of lines equals the submission total;
- activation line count matches the expected approved line count;
- activation snapshots reconcile with created operational allocations;
- financial variance required by activation is K0.00.

### 22.4 Spending integrity

Where the application's implemented accounting logic permits the equation, verify:

`Original Budget +/- Approved Revisions - Commitments - Actual Payments = Available Balance`

without double-counting commitments released/converted by payment.

### 22.5 Funding integrity

Authority, receipt, allocation and release values must reconcile under the current implemented funding rules and must not exceed their governing source/authority limits.

## 24. Auditability and dataset versioning

Every generated UAT record that supports metadata tagging should carry or be traceable to:

- dataset version `NJSS-NATIONAL-UAT-2026-V1`;
- run identifier `UAT-2026-V1-20260829` or a later date-stamped run identifier if execution occurs on a later date;
- provenance classification OFFICIAL, DERIVED or UAT where supported;
- creation actor and timestamp according to the applicable workflow.

Where an existing table has no dataset/provenance field, the implementation plan shall choose the least invasive traceability mechanism, such as a controlled description/notes field or a dedicated seed-run registry, rather than adding redundant columns to every table.

## 25. Recovery and rollback

Before the destructive reset:

- capture a fresh full database recovery snapshot using the established NJSS backup/recovery mechanism;
- verify that the snapshot is registered and has non-zero expected table/record content;
- separately record counts and identifiers for preserved users, roles and role assignments;
- export/record the exact pre-reset organisational assignments of retained users for rollback evidence;
- record baseline counts for every rebuildable table.

If any critical seed or reconciliation stage fails before acceptance, the rebuild must stop. The implementation plan shall define whether recovery is by transaction rollback, controlled reseed from the pre-reset snapshot, or the established restore pathway depending on the stage of failure.

A partially rebuilt environment must not be declared UAT-ready.

## 26. Acceptance criteria

The rebuild is complete only when all of the following are true:

1. all 10 retained user identities still exist;
2. the 7 active users remain active and the 3 archived users remain archived;
3. roles and permissions reconcile with their pre-reset preserved state except for separately approved changes;
4. 22 province-level jurisdictions exist in the UAT catalogue;
5. 28 Court Locations exist with valid Province bindings;
6. every active Department/Section/Cost Centre has valid geographic and organisational ownership;
7. retained active users are remapped to approved new organisational units;
8. all required Finance Codes used by activated budgets have explicit canonical mappings;
9. no activated line depends on a fallback Chart of Accounts record;
10. budget line, monthly-allocation, activation and operational-allocation totals reconcile;
11. at least one activated budget exists for every main National Court Registry intended by the approved UAT model;
12. representative Waigani and provincial FF3/FF4/funding/revision scenarios exist in the planned workflow-state distribution;
13. all required negative validation cases are rejected for the expected reason;
14. all positive reconciliation checks pass;
15. the dataset/run version is recorded;
16. the final Word report records sources, assumptions, installed masters, coding conventions, reset steps, user reassignment, counts, financial totals, validation evidence and any deviations.

17. dataset version/run ID;
18. exceptions, limitations and items requiring future reconciliation against official NJSS establishment/IFMS data.

## 28. Non-goals

This rebuild does not:

- claim to replace the official NJSS HR establishment;
- claim that UAT Finance Codes are official IFMS codes;
- claim that UAT budgets are real appropriations;
- change retained users' authentication credentials;
- redesign RBAC unless a separately approved issue is required for the new location hierarchy;
- activate any budget until the explicit finance mappings and dual-control workflow validations pass;
- suppress or bypass database integrity controls merely to make seeding easier.

## 29. Implementation transition

After this written specification is reviewed and approved, the next step is a separate implementation plan. That plan must define the migration, seed-generation, dependency-ordered purge, backup/rollback, workflow-aware transaction creation, validation and verification tasks in executable order.

No production/UAT business-data reset is authorised by this specification alone.
