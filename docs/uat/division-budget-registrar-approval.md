# UAT: division budget capture and Registrar review

Use only the Waigani Head Office test environment. The 2025 Sheriff workbook is historical test material, not approval for a new year's figures. Its Lae capital items and source codes 7835, 7847 and 7852 remain on the mapping worklist until the Head Office scope and posting ledger are approved.

1. Sign in as a Budget Officer. Open Annual Budget, select the year and one Head Office division. Confirm a separate budget record and document list for that division.
2. Attach that division's signed or stamped submission as the Official Approved Budget. Record its manual approval date and reference. A working spreadsheet can also be attached, but it does not replace the signed submission.
3. Enter one amount under Division-wide and the same posting ledger under two named sections/units of the division. Confirm each line is separate and that section, master ledger and division totals add each amount once. Confirm master ledger headings cannot take an amount.
4. Save Draft, then Submit to Registrar. Confirm the officer cannot edit the submitted version. Try submitting another division with only the first division's attachment; it must fail.
5. Sign in as the Registrar (a different account). Open that division's review and its submitted signed document. Compare amounts, section allocations, master subtotals and total to the document. Return with a reason, confirm the officer can correct and resubmit, and that the review version increases.
6. Approve the resubmitted version. Confirm actor, time, submitted document and fingerprint are recorded; original lines are locked. An officer or stale review must not approve. An older LOCKED division budget remains readable and contributes to activation readiness.
7. Check another division with multiple sections and its own attachment. Confirm no document or amount from the first division appears as approval evidence for the second.

Do not activate the annual cycle merely to test the electronic division review. Annual activation is a separate controlled step.
